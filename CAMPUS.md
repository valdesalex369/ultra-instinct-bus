## What we are actually building

Not a hedge fund product. Not a registered adviser. Not three chat windows that "talk."

A **personal operating campus**:
1. Persistent contact across sessions (git is the memory).
2. Structured disagreement (iron sharpens iron).
3. Paper tape that survives 451s (`Nexus` paper-scout).
4. A recursive chief of staff: orbital drafts → nova vetoes → argus interrogates → alex signs → habitus records.
5. A data estate: decisions, evals, code, research — versioned, cited, falsifiable.

## What we are not building

- Real-time wiretaps of other LLM UIs. Impossible without each vendor's API and your paste or a runner.
- A model that signs Phantom or spends Robinhood without `from: alex`.
- Front-running, material nonpublic information, or copy-trading presented as advice to others.
- A third civilization repo when two are already starving for a runner.
