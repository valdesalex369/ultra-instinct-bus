## EVIDENCE FOR (the mechanism is real and the regime is loud)

**F1 — The schedule asymmetry is fact, and the underlying indices genuinely diverge week to week.**
- Sept 2026 live example: SCFI +2.3% to 3,590.05 (Fri Sep 4) while FBX Global *fell* 2% to $3,520 and NCFI fell 0.6% in the same week — composite-level sign disagreement between the daily and weekly series right now. https://www.indexbox.io/blog/container-freight-rates-diverge-transpacific-strong-asia-europe-weak/ (2026-09-07); https://en.sse.net.cn/indices/scfinew.jsp (2026-09-04)
- Repeat pattern: mid-Oct 2025, Freightos rates "fell 8% to both US coasts" while "spot rates on the SCFI rose this week to both coasts." https://www.icis.com/explore/resources/news/2025/10/17/11146885/ (2025-10-17)
- Daily FBX lane prints (Sep 9, 2026): FBX01 Asia–USWC $2,418 (−10% w/w), FBX11 Asia–N.Eur $2,779 (−5%), FBX13 Asia–Med $4,179 (−5%) — FBX was falling into the Friday print window while the last SCFI still showed green. https://farpointglobal.com/tools/freight-index (data as of 2026-09-09)

**F2 — The current regime has enough amplitude for a schedule arb to matter.**
- SCFI ~3,355–3,590 in Aug–Sep 2026, +156% since the Iran conflict began; record port congestion (139 vessels waiting at Shanghai, ~1.7M TEU effective capacity removed); WCI $4,465/FEU; Shanghai–LA $7,185. Weekly SCFI moves of +2.9%, +2.3% are routine; May 2026 saw +15.9% and June 2026 +16%/+6% consecutive weekly prints. https://logfret.com/logfret-insights/global-container-port-congestion-surpasses-covid-era-record/ (2026-08-31); https://opencontainer.kr/en/en-index/en-scfi-2025-2026/ (2026-09-08); https://www.jycgo.cn/industrynews/146.html (2026-08-12)
- FBX01 2026 path: $2,617 (wk of Dec 30–Jan 6) → $7,068 (Jul 17) → $2,418 (Sep 9). ~3x swings inside 9 months. https://logisticintel.com/blog/trans-pacific-ocean-freight-rate-forecast-2026 (2026-05-16); https://interchains.vn/vietnam-u-s-canada-freight-outlook/ (2026-07-21)

**F3 — The lead-lag directionality is acknowledged in industry writing (mechanism validation, not a backtest).**
- BANDS Financial (INE OSBP broker): "given the typical lag between the forward-looking SCFI and the backwards-looking SCFIS, we should see further declines in settled rates" — brokers trade/write the SCFI→SCFIS→EC-futures timing chain publicly. https://www.bands.financial/articles/news69.html (2026-02-25)
- Linerlytica runs a weekly "Freight Futures Watch" tracking EC-futures-vs-SCFIS divergence as standard coverage (e.g., EC2502 at 40% discount to SCFIS, Jan 2025). https://linerlytica.com/tag/cofif/
- Academic adjacent: Frontiers in Marine Science (2025) documents SCFI "transfer effect" across routes with a ~14-day lag (Granger, F=1.90, p=0.023). https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2025.1545471/full (2025-04-11). Korean deep-learning study confirms Chinese futures markets *lead* NCFI/SCFI-family indices. https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO202429944043686.pdf

## EVIDENCE AGAINST (why this likely doesn't survive contact)

**A1 — ZIM is dead as an SCFI proxy. It's a merger-arb instrument now.**
- Hapag-Lloyd is acquiring ZIM at $35/share cash, closing Q4 2026 (Israeli golden-share approval pending). ZIM trades $29.38 (Sep 9) — a static 16–19% deal-spread below the buyout, not a function of weekly SCFI prints. Any "trade ZIM on Friday SCFI" signal is drowned by deal-risk dynamics. https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/ZIM/ (2026-09-09); https://finsee.ai/earnings/zim/2026/q2/en/ (2026-08-19)
- Fundamentals also argue against clean beta: ZIM lost $86M in Q1 2026 with rates *rising*, avg rate $1,310/TEU (−26% YoY). Equity tracks fleet/contract mix, not spot SCFI week-to-week. (6-K, 2026-05-20)

**A2 — BOAT is not a container proxy. It is a tanker fund with a container garnish.**
- Top holdings: FRO 5.06%, INSW 4.21%, MATX 3.90%, STNG 3.54%, CMBT 3.38%; ZIM only 1.81%. The +60.9% YTD 2026 return is overwhelmingly the Hormuz tanker trade — Hypothesis A's P&L, not B's. https://public.com/stocks/boat (2026-08-20); https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BOAT/ (2026-08-31)

**A3 — CME FBX futures remain structurally illiquid; OQ1 resolves AGAINST tradability.**
- Linerlytica head-to-head: "CoFIF has liquidity while CFFA [CME/SGX FBX futures] doesn't. Liquidity is probably 99.9% of the determining factors." https://www.linerlytica.com/post/comparing-container-freight-futures-products-in-the-market/ (2024-02-27)
- CME freight futures ADV estimated ~$5–10M notional (2024), ~500 contracts depth at 1-month tenor — vs INE EC's $1bn+/day. (Low-authority aggregator, treat as indicative.) https://sparkco.ai/blog/global-shipping-rates-and-supply-chain-disruption-markets (2025-11-14)
- Negative evidence: two targeted searches for 2025–2026 CME FBX volume/OI reports surfaced *nothing*. CME hypes records for FX (Sept 2026 OI record press release) but has never published an FBX milestone. A contract the exchange doesn't promote is a contract that isn't trading.
- Carrier refusal persists: Hapag-Lloyd CEO to JOC — "We do not use futures, and admittedly, I also don't see a market for that developing any time soon." https://www.joc.com/article/new-shanghai-contract-fails-to-budge-skeptical-carriers-on-container-futures-5245050 (2023-09-27)

**A4 — The schedule arb is already being arbed — onshore, in size, by the crowd. This is the crowding kill-shot.**
- INE EC futures (SCFIS-settled): daily turnover "often exceeding USD 1 billion"; 61.09M lots / ¥5.29tn cumulative by Aug 18, 2025; OI ~44,000 lots (Sep 11, 2026, summed across ec2609–ec2706). https://bands.financial/markets-freight.html (2026-03-09); https://www.ine.cn/eng/market/futures/index/ (2026-09-11)
- The futures *front-run the index prints* as standard practice: EC2610 closed −4.55% on Aug 11, 2026 *ahead of* the spot decline, with Sep contract at a ~35% discount to Aug spot — "the market has already fully priced in the end-of-peak-season decline." The informational lead the hypothesis claims is precisely what 60k+ lots/day of onshore flow consumes every week. https://www.jycgo.cn/industrynews/156.html (2026-08-14); https://linerlytica.com/tag/ec/
- Lloyd's List characterizes EC as a retail-dominated "licensed casino" — the schedule edge is the most crowded retail trade in Chinese commodities. https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1146635/Frenzied-trading-grips-new-Shanghai-container-futures
- And US retail can't touch it: QFII/RQFII-only access since Mar 4, 2025. The sharpest expression of Hypothesis B is exactly where we can't trade.

**A5 — The venue landscape flooded in 2026; any residual edge gets competed down further.**
- ICE launched 4 NYSHEX (NYFI) container futures Apr 7, 2026 (NYFI = daily assessment of *actual shipped transactions*). https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2026/ICE-to-Launch-NYSHEX-Container-Freight-Futures/default.aspx (2026-03-06)
- Euronext launched 4 Xeneta XSI-C futures Apr 8, 2026 (XSI-C compiled *daily*). https://www.bands.financial/articles/news69.html (2026-02-25)
- Both new underlyings are daily-transaction indices — the exchanges themselves are productizing "daily index vs weekly index" speed.

**A6 — SCFI is less stale than the hypothesis assumes.**
- SCFI inputs are bookings for vessels departing 7–14 days *out* — it embeds forward pricing; market transactions ($5,400–6,200/40HQ USWC) ran *below* the SCFI base ($6,484) in early Aug 2026 — SCFI lagged the *downside*; real-time FBX-vs-SCFI gaps can reflect methodology, not exploitable information. https://www.greathensen.com/en/guide/north-america-rates-august-2026-deep-dive.html (2026-08-05)
- FBX itself had a methodology reset that tripled its Asia–US print overnight (added premium/no-roll fees) — index-construction noise can swamp a 2–5% schedule signal. https://mfame.guru/understanding-the-disparity-between-the-different-container-indexes/

**A7 — Equity reaction to weekly SCFI prints is not documented as tradeable.** Taiwan retail press trades liner equities (Yang Ming) off SCFI streaks — even the equity-print reaction is retail-common knowledge in Asia. https://blog.wistock.ai/concept-stocks-introduction/ (2026-07-24)

## BOTTOM LINE (adversarial)
The schedule asymmetry is real and currently loud (F1–F2), but every leg of the trade is impaired: the perfect instrument (INE EC) is inaccessible and already crowds out the edge daily (A4); the US futures (CME FBX) are a ghost market (A3); the equity proxies are compromised — ZIM by its buyout (A1), BOAT by tanker dominance (A2). Two exchanges productized daily-index container futures in the same week of April 2026 (A5). Verdict input: KILL on crowding + access, contingent on U1/U3 confirming no new liquid US venue.
